Life-cycle assessment

NoteNo project values yet? Start with standard cases
  • Construction and materials: New-construction and refurbishment cases provide an initial material assignment. The table also contains test columns; check your selection.

Check the applied values for your project. How to apply presets.

Where in PEExcel? ☁ THG (Graue Emissionen, Ökobilanz), also called “Global Warming Potential” in the manual.

Climate-neutral PED assessment extends beyond operational energy. Embodied building emissions and operational greenhouse gas emissions are assessed over a defined study period.

Distinguishing three time concepts

Term Meaning
Study period Balance period: 50 years, 2025–2075
Target year Policy or methodological climate target, such as 2040 or 2050; not the balance end date
Decarbonisation pathway Evolution of energy-system emission factors, affecting future operational energy

A cumulative presentation integrates contributions over time. It is neither a single annual balance nor automatic proof of achieving a national climate target. Reduction pathways and budget rules belong to the assessment method.

GHG reduction pathways and life cycle

The method uses 2025–2075 and sectoral decarbonisation pathways to 2050. Decarbonised systems after 2050 are an assessment assumption. The training’s budget of 320 kg CO₂eq/m² gross floor area is a cumulative reference, not an annual emissions rate.

The timing of emissions, refurbishment and biogenic storage affects the life-cycle balance. Credits in the diagram are methodological assumptions, not universally transferable material properties.

Components, operation and budget

Assign constructions and quantities transparently. Interpret embodied and operational contributions together with boundary, period and budget. The variant comparison shows exported life-cycle results, including negative assessment contributions.

Declaration — PV LCA: Embodied PV manufacturing emissions can be modelled, but the klimaaktiv assessment method does not include them. An input or result does not automatically mean a contribution enters the declaration. Do not generalise this to other systems or methods.

Entering LCA inputs

Location: ☁ THG → Baulich (construction).

Where in PEExcel?

  1. Under Baulich, select constructions or a preset and check quantities.
  2. Add grouped measures or an overall LCA input without counting existing measures twice.
  3. Assign fossil and biogenic contributions and service life until replacement transparently.
  4. Under Technische Gebäudeausstattung (building services), enter relevant systems and reference quantities; align vehicle manufacturing with mobility settings.
  5. Recalculate and check construction and operational contributions together on the results sheet.

Component assignment connects geometry, quantities and life-cycle balance. A geometric area alone is not a complete LCA assignment. Construction, boundary conditions and use must be traceable.

Project workflow

  1. Assign areas and components according to actual construction.
  2. Distinguish outdoor air, ground, heated and unheated adjacent spaces.
  3. Check quantities and units before importing LCA properties.
  4. Document unshown elements such as balconies, columns and window frames separately.
  5. Check adopted component values and assessment boundary.
NoteResearch / Expert

In the second reference model, the basement ceiling separates heated from unheated space and forms the thermal boundary. The internal floor slab is also an assessed construction component but is not an external thermal boundary there. Thermal and life-cycle balances examine different properties of the same component.

Connecting to Eco2soft

Where in PEExcel? Create the appropriate assessment in Eco2soft and export CSV. Align system boundary, reference area, units, fossil/biogenic contributions and replacement assumptions before transfer. After importing, check which components or aggregate results were adopted and review existing inputs for double counting.

Check field mapping in the CSV import dialog. Different boundaries or already recorded components can otherwise cause missing contributions or double counting.
