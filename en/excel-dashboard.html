Excel Dashboard

Excel location: 📈 Dashboard worksheet.

The Dashboard presents hourly energy flows and storage states. A common time window allows demand, coverage and storage response to be examined together. The Python scenario explorer is a separate application.

Ribbon: klimaaktiv PEQ → Ergebnisse → Dashboard

Selecting a time window

In the Excel tool

  1. Select a period with Datum auswählen; use the timeline for a continuous range.
  2. Choose a manageable interval, such as one week, for detailed review.
  3. Check that the chart axes show hourly timestamps over the selected days.
  4. After recalculation, use Aktualisieren to update the pivot charts.

Electricity demand and use

The upper chart includes plug loads, inflexible building-services loads, EV charging and additional flexible use. Read storage charging as a separate contribution. Compare peaks with flexibility schedules and storage states.

Electricity coverage

The second chart separates conventional and flexible grid imports, PV use, battery discharge, EV discharge and PV feed-in. Use the legend to identify overlapping contributions; respect sign conventions and the balance boundary.

Room temperature and thermal building mass

Temperatures in cooled and uncooled zones indicate the thermal response. Check pre-conditioning against setpoints and permissible temperature ranges. Temperature is a state indicator, not stored energy in kWh.

Hot-water storage

The states of hot-water systems 1 and 2 show charging and use in the selected period. Read percentages against the chart scale; they are not temperatures or absolute storage capacities.

Electric vehicles and battery

EV SoC shows vehicle state of charge by use; Battery SOC shows the stationary electrical store. Assess minimum state of charge, flexible charging and discharge together with the electricity flows.

Thermal daily averages

Heat gains and losses for the two thermally decoupled zones are also presented as daily averages. Daily averages and hourly power are different aggregations.

Storage inputs · Results worksheet · Aspern F7 profiles

Website profiles use exported channels. A channel missing from an export does not imply it is absent from the Excel Dashboard.
